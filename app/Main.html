Insync

Watch a video with someone else, actually in step.

Turn on the Insync extension in Safari. The extension is on. The extension is off.

Setting up

  1. Turn on Insync in Safari Settings, under Extensions.
  2. Click the Insync button in the toolbar and choose Always Allow on Every Website.
  3. Reload any tabs you already had open.

Safari does not add extensions to pages that were open before you granted access, so that last step matters.

Watching together

  1. Open the video. Click the toolbar button, then Start a session.
  2. Send the six-character code to the person you are watching with.
  3. They open the same video, enter the code, and you are in step.

Play, pause and skip work from either side. If one of you gets a little ahead, Insync eases that side off by a few percent until it lines back up, which is far less jarring than jumping the video. If someone stops to buffer, the other waits.

What leaves your Mac

Only a room code, a playback position, and a scrambled fingerprint of the video address, used to check you are both on the same thing. The address itself is never sent, so there is no record of what you watch. There are no accounts, and rooms are forgotten when you are done.
